Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. About Manweta AI

Manweta AI ("Manweta AI", "we", "us", or "our") provides software that helps businesses work with accounting and receivables information, manage payment reminders, and use the WhatsApp Business Platform. This policy explains how we process information about our business customers and the customers of those businesses.

2. Information we process

  • Business account information: business or organisation name, account administrator details, email address, authentication information, and service configuration.
  • Customer and contact information: names, mobile numbers, and other contact details entered by a business customer or obtained through its configured accounting integration.
  • Accounting and payment information: bill or invoice references, invoice and due dates, outstanding amounts, and payment status or related receivables information.
  • WhatsApp message information: message content and history handled by the service, message identifiers, timestamps, delivery/read/failure statuses when provided by WhatsApp, and replies or other inbound messages received by the integration.
  • Integration information: WhatsApp Business Account and phone-number identifiers, template information, API response information, and credentials or tokens needed to operate the integration.
  • Technical and support information: information included in error reports, support requests, and service logs where generated.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the Manweta AI service and its accounting/receivables features.
  • Identify due or overdue bills and prepare payment reminders according to the business customer's settings.
  • Send and manage WhatsApp messages and display message status information returned by WhatsApp.
  • Store message history and related delivery status in the database so business customers can review activity and troubleshoot delivery.
  • Maintain accounts, secure the service, respond to support requests, and investigate errors or misuse.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce our terms where necessary.

4. Business customers and their customers

Our business customers decide which customer, contact, invoice, and payment information to connect or enter and how to use it in their workflows. They are responsible for ensuring they have the necessary rights and lawful basis to provide this information to Manweta AI and to contact recipients through WhatsApp. We process this information to provide the service to the relevant business customer.

5. WhatsApp and other service providers

When a business customer uses WhatsApp messaging features, relevant recipient information and message content are transmitted to Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to send or receive messages and obtain status updates. Use of WhatsApp is also subject to Meta's terms and policies. We may use hosting, database, infrastructure, and other service providers to operate Manweta AI; those providers process information as needed to provide their services to us.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain business account data, customer and accounting records, WhatsApp message history, and message status information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain the business customer's records, resolve issues, and meet applicable legal or security requirements. Retention periods may differ by data type and customer configuration. Business customers can request deletion by following our Data Deletion Instructions. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, dispute-resolution, or backup purposes for a limited period.

7. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect information. Access is limited to authorised purposes. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Details about specific safeguards should be understood in light of the controls actually implemented in the deployed service.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact support@manweta.com.
