Data Deletion Instructions

How business customers can request deletion of data stored by Manweta AI.

1. Request deletion

A business account administrator can request deletion by emailing support@manweta.com from the email address associated with the account. Use the subject Manweta AI data deletion request and include the business or account name. Please do not email passwords, access tokens, or unnecessary sensitive customer information.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the requester is authorised to make the request. We will acknowledge the request and communicate if we need additional information or if any data must be retained for legal, security, or dispute-resolution reasons.

2. Information covered by a deletion request

Subject to verification, applicable law, and any required retention, a request may cover:

  • Manweta AI business account and user information;
  • customer names, mobile numbers, and contact records stored for the business;
  • accounting records synced or entered through the service, including bill references, invoices, due dates, outstanding balances, and payment status;
  • WhatsApp message history, inbound replies, message identifiers, and stored delivery/read/failure statuses; and
  • WhatsApp integration credentials and related configuration stored by Manweta AI.

3. Disconnecting WhatsApp

If the dashboard provides a WhatsApp disconnect option, a business administrator may use it to stop the integration. Disconnecting an integration is not necessarily the same as deleting the Manweta AI account or all historical data. To request deletion of stored records, contact us using the instructions above. You can also manage or revoke access through the relevant Meta business settings.

4. Processing and limitations

We will process verified requests within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable law. Some information may be retained where required for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, resolving disputes, or maintaining limited backup records. Where data remains in backups, it will be handled under the applicable backup lifecycle and will not be restored for ordinary use except as needed for recovery or compliance.

Deleting information from Manweta AI does not delete messages already delivered to recipients' WhatsApp devices or remove records held independently by Meta, a business customer, or another third party. Those parties' own systems and policies apply to their copies.

5. Contact

For data deletion requests, email support@manweta.com.
