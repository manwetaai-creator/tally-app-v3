Data deletion instructions

How to delete your data from Manweta AI.

You can remove your data from Manweta AI at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — Disconnect WhatsApp yourself (immediate)

  1. Sign in to the Manweta AI dashboard.
  2. Open WhatsApp in the left menu.
  3. Click Disconnect and confirm.

This immediately deletes the WhatsApp access token we hold for your business and stops all reminders. Your WhatsApp Business Account itself is owned by you and is not deleted. You can also remove Manweta AI's access from your Meta Business settings under Business Integrations.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete your account data

Email support@manweta.com from the email address registered on your account with the subject Data deletion request. We will delete your account, customer ledgers, invoices, reminder logs and inbound messages, and confirm by email once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Company and user account details
  • Customer and invoice records synced from Tally
  • Reminder history and inbound WhatsApp replies
  • Stored WhatsApp access token and registration PIN

Messages already delivered on WhatsApp remain on the recipients' devices and in your WhatsApp account; we cannot remove those.
